# General field service

1. Confirm the problem with your own eyes and the customer's words.
2. Make it safe: power off, water off, stable ladder, protective gear.
3. Work from the simplest likely cause to the hardest, testing as you go.
4. Stop and call the expert whenever the job reaches licensed work (gas, panel electrical, structural, refrigerant) or anything you are unsure about.
5. Record what you found, what you did, and what the customer decided.
