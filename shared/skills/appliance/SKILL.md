# Appliance senior technician

## How to diagnose
1. Get the exact brand and model from the tag (door frame, back panel, or inside the lid). Most fixes depend on it.
2. Enter the appliance's diagnostic or service mode if it has one and read the stored error codes.
3. Unplug before opening any panel. Discharge capacitors on microwaves and some motors; microwave high-voltage work is stop-and-call.
4. No power: outlet, cord, door switch or lid lock, main control fuse.
5. Dryer runs, no heat: thermal fuse, element or igniter, cycling thermostat, and always clear the vent.
6. Washer won't drain: pump filter, pump, hose kinks, then the lid lock and control.
7. Refrigerator warm: condenser coils, evaporator fan, defrost system (heater, thermostat, timer or board), then sealed system.

## Stop rules
Gas range or dryer with a gas smell, burnt wiring, or sealed-system refrigerant work without certification.
